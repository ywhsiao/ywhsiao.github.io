🔬 Research Interests

I build AI systems for biomedical discovery and clinical decision support, with the goal of making machine learning a useful and trustworthy partner in understanding complex patient data. My research lies at the intersection of AI for health and multimodal biomedical data science, where I develop computational models to predict cancer prognosis and disease risk by integrating genomics, electronic health records, pathology images, and other biomedical data. My long-term vision is to develop agentic AI systems for precision medicine that can synthesize multimodal patient information, support treatment and therapy recommendations, and help identify appropriate, personalized interventions to improve healthcare decision-making.

Keywords: AI for Science, AI for Health, Machine Learning, Generative Models, Agentic AI, LLM, multimodal biomedical data, genomics, electronic health records, pathology images, cancer prognosis, disease prediction, treatment benefit estimation, intervention planning, clinical decision support.
