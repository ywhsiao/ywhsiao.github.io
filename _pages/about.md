Hello, I'm a fourth-year PhD candidate in Biostatistics at National Taiwan University with an expected graduation date in Jul 2023. My entire PhD period is supported by the Outstanding Doctoral Scholarship Pilot Program of MOST (Sep 2019 - Jul 2023), Taiwan. Now, I am also a visiting research student at the University of Melbourne, under the Schema of Graduate Students’ Study Abroad Program of MOST (Jul 2022 - Jun 2023), Taiwan.

Before starting my PhD study, I worked as a research assistant in a Bioinformatics lab. In this role, I applied many bioinformatics pipelines to convert various kinds of next-generation sequencing genomic data into a readable matrix and conducted basic statistics methods on a such matrix to unveil novel biological mechanisms across many species. Such 3+ years of hands-on research experience working on data preprocessing for sequencing genomic data at different genomic levels made me wonder how can we learn from these data to gain more biological insights or clinical applications. So, my PhD research has mainly focused on the interface of cancer genomics and its clinical applications by applying various computational methods from statistics and bioinformatics. Studying in a biostatistics group in the Department of Epidemiology provides good training for me to know how to link academic research and real molecular diagnosis to tackle different types of cancer. During this 3-year doctoral training, I also realized merging genomic data for a specific research topic is a powerful way to gain novel biological insights, such as better cancer subtype characterization. However, tacking the technological difference across different studies from different sequencing technologies. Now, I'm visiting UoM for one year to acquire knowledge about the batch effect and apply them to a real case study.

During my free time, I love traveling and sports. I have visited more than 10 countries/regions. I usually work out in the gym. I also enjoy a wide variety of activities, including hiking, boxing, and bouldering.
